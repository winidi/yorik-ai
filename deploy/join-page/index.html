Welcome to Yorik

Your family's Yorik lives at home. Three steps and your phone can reach it, at home and on the go.

1 Get the Tailscale app

Tailscale is a free app that builds a private line between your phone and your family's Yorik. Nobody else can use that line.

Get Tailscale

Open it once and sign in with your Google, Apple or Microsoft account.

2 Accept the invitation

This lets your phone see only Yorik, nothing else in the house.

Accept in Tailscale

Ask the person who invited you to share Yorik with you in Tailscale. Then come back here.

Make sure Tailscale is switched on.

3 Open Yorik

Looking for Yorik…
Open Yorik

This page checks every few seconds and opens Yorik by itself once it can reach it.
