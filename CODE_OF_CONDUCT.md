# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

We want Yorik's community — Issues, PRs, Discussions, Discord, Matrix — to be a place where contributors and users from any background feel welcome. The full text of our standards is at the link above; the short version:

- Be patient and welcoming with newcomers
- Critique code, not people
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions
- No personal attacks, no doxxing, no off-topic political fights
- Disagreement is fine — disrespect is not

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **[hi@yorik.ai](mailto:hi@yorik.ai)** with subject line `[CoC] Yorik`.

Reports are confidential. We respond within 7 days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may, at their discretion:

- Edit or remove comments / commits / PRs
- Temporarily or permanently ban contributors
- Lock threads that have stopped being productive

The full Contributor Covenant text describes the four-tier enforcement ladder we follow (correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban).
