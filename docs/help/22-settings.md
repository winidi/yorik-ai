# Settings at a glance

New here? Start with **Start here**.

Settings has a checklist at the top and three groups below it. You only see the pages your role needs: children see Profile and Sign-in & devices, adults see the You group and Letters & invoices, admins see everything.

## Start here (admins)

A checklist of what to set up: the AI model, inviting the family, backups, the extra apps, reaching Yorik from outside, your letterhead. Each line opens the place where it is done and ticks itself off.

## You

- **Profile** — your name, what the family calls you, language (English or Deutsch), country, address and phone, your colour and photo, and the theme (Light, Dark or System).
- **Sign-in & devices** — change your password; your four-digit PIN (for phone sign-in after a QR invite and for the family wall); your voice sample for the family wall; where you are signed in. Admins make the current device the family wall here.
- **Privacy & safety** — who may see what of yours, per area (this is also where calendars are shared in the family); who sees new documents by default; whether your day and your photos show on the family wall; emergency access.
- **Assistant & alerts** — push notifications, the Undo card after Yorik changes something, suggestions, planning rules for "Plan my day", and the voice confirmation. Folded at the bottom: **Your own AI agent** (your agent, API tokens, whether agents may confirm deletions, developer mode).

## Household

- **People** (admins) — invite someone with a QR code or add them by hand, set the role (Adult, Child or Admin), pause access, remove. Folded: **Groups**, extra shared spaces such as a business.
- **Apps & accounts** (admins) — switch the extra apps on or off (Write, WhatsApp, Recordings, Pipelines, Finance). **Connections** shows where email, calendar, WhatsApp, bank and phone photos are connected; each lives in its own app. Also the maps key (OpenRouteService).
- **Letters & invoices** (all adults) — business details (business name, tax number, IBAN), your signature, your letterhead, and invoice and quote numbers. Admins also install e-invoices (ZUGFeRD) here; it works right away, no restart.
- **Backup & storage** (admins) — backups to a USB drive: set them up, see the status, run one now, choose what is included. **Where the photos live** moves the photo library to an external drive (classic install only).

## Maintenance (admins, folded)

- **Health & updates** — status at a glance (AI model, Email, Documents, Backup), updates, phone access from outside the house through Tailscale, repairs such as reconnecting documents, and the background workers.
- **AI** — the chat model and how to use a different one; speech recognition (Parakeet recommended, Whisper, Groq or another cloud service); search by meaning, with the document index and the autotagger folded below.
- **Developer** — logs, quality, skills and add-ons (the marketplace and installed apps).
- **Hosted families** (owner only) — a separate Yorik for another family on this computer.

## Not finding something?

Some things live in their own app, not in Settings: mail accounts in Email, the WhatsApp link in WhatsApp, bank accounts in Finance, calendar imports in Calendar. Or ask Yorik in chat, for example *"Where do I change my PIN?"*.
