# Schreiben — letters with your own letterhead

Schreiben is the small app for letters. (Invoices and quotes follow; until then they stay in Compose.) It is switched on under **Settings → Apps → Schreiben**. While it is on, the chat writes letters with it and Compose's chat skills rest, so that Yorik does not have two tools for the same thing; the Compose app itself stays where it is.

## Your letterhead

**Settings → Profile → Your letterhead**: logo, accent colour, font, sender, the footer with bank and tax numbers, the closing. It starts from your profile. Every letter you write looks like this, whoever wrote the text. Everyone in the household has their own.

## Let Yorik write

In the chat: "Schreib der Hausverwaltung Müller, dass die Heizung seit Montag kalt ist." Yorik writes the whole letter and shows a card; **Öffnen und bearbeiten** takes you to the sheet. Yorik does not ask for details first. The address comes from your contacts when the recipient is in them; if something is missing, the card and the sheet mark it ("Adresse fehlt") and you fill it in there. "Mach ihn freundlicher" in the same conversation changes the same draft.

## On the sheet

Left your drafts and what has left the house, in the middle the letter, right the page as it will be printed. Type a name under **An** and pick a contact, or type the address by hand. Everything is saved a moment after you type. **Yorik, überarbeite** below the text rewrites the marked passage, or the whole letter when nothing is marked (friendlier, shorter, more determined, in English); the undo arrow brings the old text back. If your text already ends with a greeting, leave "Grußformel anhängen" off.

## PDF, send, file

- **PDF** opens the letter as it will be printed.
- **Senden** mails the PDF from one of your own mail accounts.
- **Ablegen** puts the PDF into Paperless under your name, visible to you, the parents or the family.

A letter that was sent or filed is **final**: it stays what its PDF says. **Als neuen Entwurf** makes a copy to go on from. Drafts can be deleted, final letters are kept.

## Who sees what

Your letters and your letterhead are yours alone; an admin sees nothing extra. Children write letters too (to Oma, to the school).
