# Schreiben — letters, invoices and quotes

Schreiben is the small app for letters, invoices and quotes. It is switched on under **Settings → Apps → Schreiben**. While it is on, the chat writes with it and Compose's chat skills rest, so that Yorik does not have two tools for the same thing; the Compose app itself stays where it is.

## Your letterhead

**Settings → Profile → Your letterhead**: logo, accent colour, font, sender, the footer with bank and tax numbers, the closing. It starts from your profile. Every letter you write looks like this, whoever wrote the text. Everyone in the household has their own.

## Let Yorik write

In the chat: "Schreib der Hausverwaltung Müller, dass die Heizung seit Montag kalt ist." Yorik writes the whole letter and shows a card; **Öffnen und bearbeiten** takes you to the sheet. Yorik does not ask for details first. The address comes from your contacts when the recipient is in them; if something is missing, the card and the sheet mark it ("Adresse fehlt") and you fill it in there. "Mach ihn freundlicher" in the same conversation changes the same draft.

## On the sheet

Left your drafts and what has left the house, in the middle the letter, right the page as it will be printed. Type a name under **An** and pick a contact, or type the address by hand. Everything is saved a moment after you type. **Yorik, überarbeite** below the text rewrites the marked passage, or the whole letter when nothing is marked (friendlier, shorter, more determined, in English); the undo arrow brings the old text back. If your text already ends with a greeting, leave "Grußformel anhängen" off.

## PDF, send, file

- **PDF** opens the letter as it will be printed.
- **Senden** mails the PDF from one of your own mail accounts.
- **Ablegen** puts the PDF into Paperless under your name, visible to you, the parents or the family.

A letter that was sent or filed is **final**: it stays what its PDF says. **Als neuen Entwurf** makes a copy to go on from. Drafts can be deleted, final letters are kept.

## Invoices and quotes

**Neu → Rechnung** or **Angebot**, or in the chat: "Rechnung an Mustermann: Wartung pauschal 180 Euro und 2,5 Stunden zu 68." Yorik hands over the customer and the line items; the app computes the sums (Yorik never does the arithmetic) and marks what is missing. You type into the table: description, quantity, unit, net price, and a VAT rate if it is not the usual 19 %. A small business (§ 19 UStG, set in the letterhead) charges no VAT and says so on the page.

An invoice needs what § 14 UStG asks for; the box above the sheet lists what is still missing (the customer's address, the date of the service, your tax number in the letterhead …). A quote needs only a customer and a line.

**Fertigstellen** is a step of its own. Only then the document gets its number (invoices 2026-001, 2026-002 …, quotes A-2026-001 …, per person, restarting every year; the series is made the first time you need it) and today's date, the PDF is kept, and nothing changes any more. A number is taken only when everything else has worked, so a failure never leaves a gap. A draft invoice cannot be sent or filed. A finished quote has **In Rechnung umwandeln**: a draft invoice with the same customer and lines.

### E-invoice (Germany)

An invoice from a German letterhead becomes an e-invoice (ZUGFeRD / Factur-X, profile EN 16931): a PDF/A-3 that carries the invoice data inside, built from the very figures on the page and checked before you get it (schema, and every sum added up again from the finished XML). This needs the extension **ZUGFeRD** (Settings → Extensions → Install). Without it, or if the check fails, Yorik says so when you finalise and you decide whether a plain PDF will do — nothing falls back silently, and the finished invoice shows which of the two it is. Private customers need no e-invoice; business customers in Germany will from 2027/2028.

## Who sees what

Your documents and your letterhead are yours alone; an admin sees nothing extra. Children write letters (to Oma, to the school), not invoices or quotes.
